# Cloud Compliance Evidence Owner Guide

## Purpose

This synthetic training aid explains how control owners should provide review-ready evidence for a cloud compliance program.

## Evidence quality standard

Evidence should identify the control, environment, owner, collection date, covered period, system scope, and approval status. Screenshots should show relevant dates and scope. Reports and exports should be stored through an approved access-controlled channel.

## Review workflow

1. Confirm the control expectation and applicable systems.
2. Collect evidence from the authoritative source.
3. Validate completeness, period, scope, and consistency.
4. Record the owner and next review date.
5. Flag missing, partial, stale, or contradictory evidence.
6. Assign remediation, target date, and escalation path.
7. Retain the final evidence and review decision for the audit trail.

## Cloud and SaaS examples

- AWS and GCP: identity, logging, encryption, configuration, and vulnerability evidence.
- GitHub: branch protection, access, review, change, and repository configuration evidence.
- Okta: multifactor authentication, privileged access, group membership, and access-review evidence.
- Endpoints: inventory, vulnerability, configuration, and remediation evidence.
- SaaS services: access, security configuration, vendor assurance, and incident-management evidence.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate when evidence is missing for a high-criticality control, system scope is unclear, a control is not operating as expected, remediation will miss its target date, a contractual commitment may be affected, or risk acceptance exceeds the owner’s authority.

## Communication approach

Explain the business impact, control expectation, evidence gap, proposed remediation, accountable owner, target date, and residual risk. Avoid claiming certification readiness or making legal conclusions without the appropriate assessor, Legal, Security, or leadership review.
